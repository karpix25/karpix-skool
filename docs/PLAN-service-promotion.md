# PLAN: Service Promotion & Growth Strategy

**Goal**: Rapidly grow the platform among owners of paid Telegram communities.

---

## 🛑 Phase 0: Socratic Gate (Product Context)
> [!IMPORTANT]
> To finalize this strategy, we need clarity on:

1. **The "North Star" Value**: Is it the Gamification (levels/XP) or the UI/UX of the Mini App that "wows" users most?
2. **Target Niche**: Are we focusing on high-ticket masterminds or mass-market digital products?
3. **Barrier to Entry**: What is the #1 reason a community owner would NOT switch from their current setup (e.g., pinned messages) to our platform?

---

## 📈 Initial Growth Tactics (Draft)

### 1. The "Powered By" Viral Loop
- Add a subtle "Powered by [YourService]" badge in the student Mini App.
- Every student is a potential community owner.

### 2. High-Touch Onboarding for "Alpha" Tenants
- Select 5 influential community owners.
- Offer "White Glove" setup (we migrate their content for free).
- Use their case studies for social proof.

### 3. "The Golden Bridge": Automated Migration
- **AI-Import Tool**: Develop a feature where the bot scans the last 100-200 messages (or pinned messages) in a group.
- **Auto-Structuring**: Use LLM to identify "Lesson" patterns and automatically create Draft lessons in our platform.
- **Why**: This removes the #1 psychological barrier to switching.

### 4. Hybrid Launch Strategy
- Don't ask them to move *everything*.
- Strategy: "Keep your old chat, but host the *New Cohort* or *VIP content* in the Mini App."
- Result: Low friction, immediate value.

---

## 🏁 Phase X: Success Metrics
- **CAC** (Customer Acquisition Cost) vs **LTV** (Lifetime Value).
- **Time to First Lesson**: How fast an admin goes from "Setup" to "First Student".
- **Viral Coefficient**: Number of new potential admins coming from existing school students.
